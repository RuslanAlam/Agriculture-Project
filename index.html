Welcome to Soy Story

By Ruslan Alam · AG BM 170

This website is my crop report for AG BM 170. The crop I am following is the soybean. Most of us never see one, but soybeans are all around us: in cooking oil, salad dressing, tofu, soy milk, protein bars, and the feed that raises most of the chicken and pork we eat.

4.27 billionbushels of soybeans grown in the U.S. in 2022 (USDA-NASS)
96%of U.S. soybean acres use herbicide-tolerant seed (USDA-ERS)
9,000 yearssince people in China started using soybeans (National Geographic)
